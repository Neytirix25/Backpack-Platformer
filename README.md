# Backpack Platformer

**Backpack Sanctuary** is a side-scrolling roguelike about raising a team of animal companions. Explore 30 floors of a dark-fantasy world (a dungeon, the burning depths and the enchanted Fey Wilds), run into wild animals to start turn-based battles, and collect their babies and eggs to grow your Sanctuary. Everything you pack in your 10×4 backpack powers up your pets.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser (double-click works). No install or build step needed.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| A / D | Move |
| W | Jump |
| S | Drop through ledges |
| Shift | Dash |
| E | In the Sanctuary: use / pet an animal |
| B | Open the backpack |
| T | Your pets: every pet you own, swap your team, talents (also the button by your team cards) |
| 1–6 / mouse | Battle: pick an ability, then click a target |
| R / right-click | Rotate an item while packing · cancel a target in battle |
| N | Toggle the minimap |
| P / Esc | Pause menu: resume, restart the run, return to the Sanctuary or the title screen |
| M | Music off / all sound off / on |

## How it works

- **Your first companion:** a new game starts in the Sanctuary, where you pick a Shepherd Pup, a Fox Kit or a Cockatiel Chick.
- **Wild animals:** each floor has wild animal groups roaming its ledges. Run into one and a turn-based battle starts: your hero stands at the back, your pets line up front, middle and back, and the wild animals face them, **as many foes as you have pets**. Groups come in different compositions (packs, shield walls, healer-backed, hexers, venom), and Alphas and the animals of Acts II and III know ultimate abilities.
- **Turn-based battles:** each pet picks one of its abilities (damage, heals, shields, buffs and curses such as Burn, Poison, Bleed, Stun, Blind, Weaken and Mark), then the foes act. The hero can use one backpack consumable per turn (heal, revive, cure, Strength, smoke, net). Long fights tire everyone out. If all your active pets faint, the run ends, but your pets keep every level.
- **Three acts of animals:** floors 1–10 are home to ordinary animals (Shepherd, cats, Cockatiel, Sunday, Fox, Owl, Ferret, Boar, Toad). Floors 11–20 belong to mythical beasts only: the Dragon, the Shadow Wolf and eight fire beasts — the **Phoenix** (healer that rises from its ashes), **Hellhound** (burning bites, Three Heads), **Salamander** (stacks burns, regrows its tail), **Magma Tortoise** (molten-shell guardian), **Qilin** (sacred blessings), **Chimera** (lion, goat and serpent heads), **Sunmane Lion** (blazing roars) and **Ember Moth** (blinding spark dust). Each has six abilities, three talent trees and its own baby or egg, and new synergies: Hellfire (Hellhound + Phoenix), Pride (Lion + Chimera) and Magma Flow (Salamander + Tortoise). Floors 21–30, **the Fey Wilds**, belong to nine fey creatures — the **Moss Bear** (mossy guardian), **Fairy Dragon** (sleep dust and glamour), **Wolpertinger** (antlered, winged hare), **Moon Stag** (the healer: silver moonlight), **Bramble Viper** (blooming venom and vines), **Cu Sith** (the fairy hound whose third bay freezes foes), **Puca** (a shapeshifting thief), the **Rhino** (a thick-hided guardian whose rare shiny is a spirit rhino) and the **Spirit Wolf** (the Beastmaster's old ghostly companion) — and the Elder Dragon now roams wild there. New synergies: The Wild Hunt (Cu Sith + Spirit Wolf), Fairy Ring (Fairy Dragon + Puca) and Old Growth (Moss Bear + Bramble Viper).
- **The Fey Wilds:** three new areas — the Thornwood, the Glimmer Glade and the Moonlit Lake — with their own music, thorn brambles that hurt, bounce mushrooms that fling you about 10 tiles into the air, and vines you climb (hold W to climb, S to slide down).
- **Offspring:** beating a species you don't own always leaves its baby (Shepherd, cats, Fox, Ferret, Boar, Rhino) or its egg (Cockatiel, Sunday, Owl, Dragon, Toad). Eggs take up backpack space and hatch after 2 more wins. Species you already own sometimes leave another, which turns into experience.
- **Your collection is saved forever:** pets keep their levels (up to 30) and talents between runs. Bring any 3 into a run (front, middle, back) and swap them at the pet beds in the Sanctuary or from the backpack screen.
- **Talent trees:** every pet has three trees that read top to bottom (tiers open at levels 1, 5, 10, 16 and 22). Talents upgrade its abilities, add curses to its hits and power up the whole team, and each tree ends in an ultimate battle ability. Resetting is free.
- **Bosses:** the **Shadow Wolf** guards floor 10 (its shades vanish when it falls) the **Elder Dragon** floor 20, and the final boss, the **Lake Wyrm**, rises from the Moonlit Lake on floor 30 with its fey court. Beat the Shadow Wolf within three turns to earn the achievement and its pup; beat the Elder Dragon without a pet fainting to earn its egg; beat the Lake Wyrm and its Wyrmling joins your Sanctuary.
- **The Unicorn:** defeat a whole enemy team on the first turn of a battle and a Unicorn, impressed by your strength, joins your Sanctuary.
- **Clear tooltips:** hover any ability in battle to see exactly what it does with that pet's numbers: targets, damage, healing, every status with its chance and duration, and the cooldown.
- **Every animal is one of a kind:** each offspring is a new animal with its own level and talents, a **nature** (12 of them, e.g. Brave: +10% attack, -10% defense), **attack, defense and health** rolled from worst to best (grades F to S, -15% to +15%), a sex, and a **bond** that starts at 0 and grows to 100: from battles won together, petting it in the Sanctuary, feeding it Pet Treats (drop them on a pet in the team box) and finishing runs. The higher the bond, the more it dodges and the more often its attacks land through misses and dodges. You can bring two of the same species into a run.
- **Looks (the Wardrobe mirror):** each class has its own body type, outfit, hairstyle, hair colour and skin tone, with a big animated preview. Every class has its classic outfit plus three act outfits (Warrior: Stone Knight, Flameforged, Thornwarden · Archer: Dungeon Scout, Ember Ranger, Glade Stalker · Rogue: Shadow Cutpurse, Ashblade, Moonveil · Beastmaster: Kennel Keeper, Drakehide, Wildheart), each earned by beating that act's boss. 13 hairstyles (6 free, the rest earned: find a shiny, breed an animal, beat 100 wild animals, win a friend battle, trade an animal, befriend 15 species, reach floor 21), 6 hair colours and 6 skin tones. The classic outfits keep their hoods and helmets (hair hidden; the skin tone tints the face). Friends see your look. (This replaced the old outfit dyes.)
- **Save files:** your progress is saved in your browser as you play. To move it to another browser or computer (or between the online version and a folder copy), open the board in the Records Hall and press **Export save file**; in the other copy, press **Import save file** and pick that file. Importing replaces that copy's progress, after asking first.
- **The pond:** walk all the way left in the Yard to a pond in the ground with a wooden dock you can walk out on (step off it and you splash back onto the dock). Fish swim about as ripples on the water. Fish off the end of the dock (E or tap): a target sweeps over the water, tap to cast there; a fish near your bait swims over and bites, so aim at the ripples. Tap in time, then stop the marker in the green to reel it in (rarer fish fight harder). Dock art: `tools/dock.py`.
- **The fish bag:** 12 kinds of fish in 5 rarities (common to legendary: minnow, bluegill, crawdad, perch, sunfish, carp, pike, catfish, pufferfish, eel, sturgeon, the Golden Koi), each a different shape. They go into the fish bag, a 7 x 5 packing grid like the backpack (the creel at the foot of the dock, C, or Fish bag in the touch menu): drag and rotate them to fit, drop one on the wave to let it go. A catch that doesn't fit waits in the tray. The bag keeps a log of everything you've caught. Fish are ingredients, for cooking later. Art: `tools/fish.py`.
- **Cooking:** a stone oven (glowing embers, steam from its chiminea) stands in the Yard between the house and the Menagerie. Its menu shows your fish bag: pick a fish and it goes in the oven; take it out while the heat bar is in the golden zone for a Golden dish (too soon: underdone; a little late: well done; too late: charred). Rarer fish cook faster with a narrower golden zone. Each fish makes its own dish, the same shape as the fish, and dishes go into the **Sanctuary bag** (I or B in the Sanctuary, or the menu's Backpack): another 7 x 5 packing grid. Walk up to one of your animals and press E (or tap) to open the bag next to it: drop a dish on it to feed it (experience and bond; rarer fish and better cooking give more), or give it a pat. Opened on its own, the bag lets you feed your team. Art: `tools/cook.py`.
- **Accounts:** log in with a username and password (the Account button on the title screen or the Records board) and your progress is kept online too (Firebase), so it follows you to any browser or device. It saves by itself a few seconds after anything changes. The first time an account meets a browser that already has progress, you choose what to keep, and the other save is downloaded as a file first, so nothing is wiped; a save from another device is never overwritten without asking. There's no e-mail, so a forgotten password can't be reset. The database rules are in `firestore.rules`.
- **Play with a friend (online):** walk up to the friend post in the Yard (the mailbox with the pigeon) or press F. One of you hosts a room and reads out its 5-letter code; the other joins with it. Then you can:
  - **Battle:** your teams fight with their real levels, talents, natures and grades, plus each hero's class bonus (no items). Win or lose, your team gains bond, and the result goes in the Records Hall. You can give up from the pause menu.
  - **Trade:** each of you offers one animal; when you both accept, they swap. A traded animal keeps its level, talents and traits, but its bond starts again at 0. Animals in the Breeding Den can't be traded.
  - **Visit:** walk around your friend's Yard and meet their animals while you both see each other move, live. The friend post takes you home.
  - It connects through PeerJS, a free public service that introduces the two browsers; after that they talk directly. You need to be online, and some school or work networks block it.
- **The Sunday:** a Sun conure x Jenday parrot that lives in the Stone Hall and Mossy Cave (floors 1-10) and leaves an egg. A Support that gives the team every kind of buff: Strength, Luck, Shields, Guard, Regen, Evade and Undying, and cures curses. When it stands still it nibbles a walnut held in its foot; its shiny is sky-blue, green and pink, and sparkles like every other shiny.
- **The Rhino:** a white rhino that roams the Glimmer Glade and Moonlit Lake (floors 21-30) and leaves a calf. A Guardian: **Stomp**, **Gore** (makes the foe Bleed) and **Charge**, which takes two turns: it lowers its horn (Charging), then on its next turn hits every foe. Stunning it while it charges breaks the charge. Talent trees: **Thick Hide** (takes less damage from Bleeding foes), **Stampede** (Charge damage, less damage taken while Charging) and **Bloodhorn** (extra bleed damage). Its shiny is a spirit-blue rhino with gold horns, drawn as its own sheet.
- **Release / Donate:** in the Sanctuary, the team board's **Your Pets** screen has a Release / Donate button. Click any number of animals to pick them, then **Release** them back to the wild (nothing in return) or **Donate** them to a good home (your team pets share experience: a quarter of what it took to raise each one). Pets in your team and the pair in the Breeding Den can't be picked, and giving up a shiny asks twice.
- **Depth backgrounds:** every dungeon area, the camp and the boss chamber is drawn in three layers (far, middle, near) that slide at different speeds as you move, so the backdrop has real depth. They line up exactly like the original picture at the middle of the level. The Sanctuary Yard has its own three distances: a castle on a far hill under the mountains, green fields in the middle and a wooden fence along the ground, each sliding at its own speed as you walk, with a seamless strip of grass and wildflowers growing up in front of everything.
- **Living backgrounds:** every area moves: lava pours down the Forge's falls and embers rise, waterfalls flow in the Mossy Cave, Glimmer Glade and Moonlit Lake, the moon path glitters, the Sanctum's rune circle swirls, fog drifts through the Crypt, Hollow, Sanctum, Thornwood and boss chamber, the Stone Hall's and the boss chamber's banners sway, fireflies, dust motes and butterflies float about, and clouds drift over the Lake and the Yard. The Moonlit Lake's water and reflections ripple and its willows' hanging strands sway. Battles use the same moving backdrops.
- **Status icons:** every buff and debuff in battle shows as an icon above the unit's HP bar (red frame = debuff, gold = buff) with its turns left, Shield points or dodges in the corner. Hover over an icon, or click it to pin it, for its full rules with the real numbers.
- **Roaming animals:** wild animals wander their stretch of floor, rest and look about, and come over to sniff at you when you're near, instead of pacing back and forth.
- **Act III battle intro:** curtains of hanging vines unroll from the top like drapes, sway, then are drawn apart from the middle; climbable vines hang right from their ledges.
- **Act IV, the Drowned Deep (floors 31-40):** the Sunken Temple, the Coral Grotto and the Abyssal Trench, under the sea (drips and bubbles, no swimming), with a crashing-wave battle intro. Six amphibious creatures live there: the Kelpie, Kappa, Axolotl, Coral Crab, Hydra and Selkie. On floor 40 waits the Runeshell Leviathan, an ancient turtle whose shell blazes with runes; beat it and its hatchling joins you.
- **The Menagerie:** a new hall off the Yard showing every species on four tiers of ledges, one per act; the ones you haven't found are silhouettes (stand by one for how to get it). Five of your animals play in the Yard: choose which at the doghouse (where the campfire was), or let it pick five at random; the team post stands by the gate.
- **Play on a phone or tablet:** no on-screen buttons except a menu button (backpack, pets, map, pause, music, back, fullscreen). In the world, drag anywhere to move (up jumps and climbs, down drops), flick sideways to dash, tap to use what's nearby; while one finger is moving you, a second finger can tap to jump or flick to dash (in the direction you flick, even against the way you're walking). In menus and battles, tap to click, drag to move items, swipe lists to scroll; hold a finger to see what's under it, and tap a second finger while holding to right-click. Works held sideways or upright: held upright, every screen has its own tall layout at full size (the world in a square at the top with your team, the floor and a full-width map under it; battles with the foes on a back row and big move buttons; stacked menus), so nothing shrinks.
- **Shinies:** about 1 in 50 offspring is a **shiny**, a rare recolour with a sparkle.
- **Breeding Den:** pair a male and a female of one species; after your next run their egg hatches. The baby's stats are its parents' average plus up to 10, so breeding your best animals makes even better ones. Shiny chance: 90% with two shiny parents, 40% with one, 2% with none.
- **Pick your starting act:** once a run has reached floor 11 (or 21), the Sanctuary gate asks where to go: start from the top, or jump straight into Act II or Act III with your team (with just your class kit in the backpack).
- **Into battle:** running into wild animals pops a '!' and freezes the world, then an act-themed wipe tears the screen away (crumbling stone in Act I, a wall of flame in Act II, vine curtains and petals in Act III) and a VS card shows both sides. Bosses get their own entrance with a title card and a roar. Press Enter or click to skip.
- **Battle music:** each act has its own battle theme, each boss its own theme with a wilder second half at half health, plus a fanfare when you win and a sting when you lose.
- **Classes shape your team:**
  - **Warrior (Vanguard):** your front pet has +30% HP and +2 defense and starts every battle shielded.
  - **Archer (Volley):** you open each battle with arrows that hit every foe, and pets get +8% crit.
  - **Rogue (Poacher):** more offspring, faster-hatching eggs, and your pets' curses last longer.
  - **Beastmaster (Bond):** pets earn 50% more experience, and you can use two items per turn.
- **Awkward shapes:** almost nothing is a single square any more — horseshoes, wishbones, rings with holes, S and Z pieces, H-shaped wind chimes and crossed hex charms. The holes inside a shape are real: smaller items can sit in them.
- **Pet items:** collars, chew bones, harnesses and charms; family items for canines, cats, birds, drakes, toads and critters; front and back position items; legendaries like the Dragon Heart; and battle consumables. Items merge up to level V.
- **Team synergies:** Clowder (both cats), Pack Hunt (Shepherd + Fox) and Wildfire (Dragon + Toad).
- **Long levels:** chasms you dash across and walls you dash through to smash them for good (each act has its own: a wooden barricade, a wall of fire, a thorn thicket, a coral and rune wall), hazards that hurt your team, challenge courses with treasure chests, and buff plants that power up your next battle. Beat half of a floor's wild groups to open the exit, or all of them for the floor's chest. Camps on floors 5 and 15 fully heal your team.
- **Hard sections (Acts III-IV):** each level has 2-3 tough stretches that end in a gold chest: mazes where you climb up and drop down to find the way out (often through a smash wall), precision jumps across single stones or crumbling blocks over spikes and brambles, gliding platforms over pits and lifts up tall walls. The chest holds a better-than-common item, gives your team experience and a buff plant for the next battle, and sometimes a caged animal waits beside it to be set free (it joins your Sanctuary). Art: `tools/dashwalls.py`.
- **A hand-made adventurer's look:** every menu is stitched leather, brass rivets, parchment notes and wooden buttons, with crisp pixel fonts (Jersey 10, Micro 5 and the blackletter Jacquarda Bastarda 9, all under the SIL Open Font License).
- **Animated abilities:** every ability has its own animation — Starfall rains stars on the foes, Howl sends sound waves rolling across the field, fireballs fly, claws slash, healing songs float notes to your pets, curses flash their runes. Damage numbers pop the moment each attack lands.
- **Original chiptune soundtrack and sound effects,** synthesized live with Web Audio. Every battle ability has its own animal voice: the Shepherd howls, bites come with a growl, the Cockatiel squawks and whistles its songs, the Toad croaks, the Owl hoots and shrieks, and the dragons roar.

## Project layout

```
index.html          the whole game (plain JavaScript + canvas, no libraries)
assets/             processed pixel art: items, characters, props, tiles, backgrounds, animations, the UI kit (assets/ui)
assets/fonts/       the bundled pixel fonts and their OFL licenses
tools/pixelate.py   turns raw art sheets into game sprites (Pillow)
tools/animate.py    slices animation sheets into frames + anims.js
```

The art was generated with ChatGPT in a dark-fantasy pixel style, then processed by the tools above. The raw sheets aren't included in the repo; the game only needs `assets/`.
